# CortexCare

## Vision

CortexCare is an AI-powered Clinical Workflow Platform that helps healthcare professionals spend less time documenting patient information and more time treating patients.

Instead of replacing doctors, CortexCare acts as an intelligent clinical assistant that automates patient intake, organizes medical conversations into structured clinical context, and preserves longitudinal patient history across visits.

## Problem Statement

In many healthcare settings, clinicians spend a significant amount of time collecting patient history, reviewing previous consultations, and documenting conversations before treatment can begin. This administrative workload reduces the time available for meaningful patient care and often results in fragmented or inconsistent clinical records.

Patients also repeat the same information across multiple visits, while doctors manually search through previous notes to understand medical history and progress.

Existing AI chatbots can answer health-related questions, but they do not manage the clinical workflow, preserve structured patient history, or provide healthcare professionals with organized context before consultation.

CortexCare addresses this problem by automating patient intake, organizing conversations into structured clinical context, and maintaining a longitudinal patient history that supports clinicians without replacing their medical judgment.
